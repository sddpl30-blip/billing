Mani Vista Residents Association

Privacy policy

Last updated 24 October 2026

This portal is run by Mani Vista Residents Association (“the Association”) for its members and committee. It keeps the Association's maintenance billing, receipts and accounts. This page says what it holds about you, why, and who can see it.

What the portal holds

  • Your account: your e-mail address, the roles the committee has given you, and when you last signed in. If a password is set for your account, only a one-way hash of it is stored, never the password.
  • Your flat and owner record: the flat number and area, your name, and any phone number or PAN the office has recorded for billing.
  • Your dues: the bills raised for your flat, payments and receipts, and any credit or debit notes.
  • Helpdesk requests you raise, and the replies to them.
  • An audit trail of changes made by committee accounts (for example, who approved a rate), as the Association's accounts require.

Signing in with Google

If you choose “Sign in with Google”, the portal asks Google only to confirm your e-mail address (the openid and email scopes). It does not read your contacts, mail, files or any other Google data, and it does not create an account: it signs you in only if the Association has already added your e-mail address.

Cookies

The portal uses only the cookies it needs to work: one that keeps you signed in, one that protects your requests against forgery, and a short-lived one during Google sign-in. There are no advertising or analytics cookies, and no tracking by third parties.

Why it is used, and who sees it

  • It is used only to run the Association's billing, collections, accounts and helpdesk.
  • A member sees only their own flat's bills and payments. Committee members see what their role needs; for example, the treasurer sees the accounts.
  • It is not sold, rented or shared for marketing. It may be shown to the Association's auditor, or disclosed where the law requires.

Where it is kept

The portal is hosted on Vercel and its database on Neon (PostgreSQL), both cloud services whose servers may be outside India. Connections to the portal and to the database are encrypted.

How long it is kept

Billing and accounting records are kept for as long as the Association must keep its books. When you leave, your account is deactivated and its sessions are ended; the records of bills and payments for the flat stay, because the accounts need them.

Your choices

You can ask the Association's committee to see the information held about you, to correct it, or to change the e-mail address you sign in with. Raise it through the Helpdesk in the portal or with the Association's office.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new date.
